## About the seminar {#about}

We are bringing together researchers who test and verify concurrent and distributed systems. We will share recent results and discuss open problems in model checking, runtime and predictive analysis, weak memory, and tools for finding concurrency bugs.

This meeting is part of [ACM India’s IndiCS seminar series](https://india.acm.org/research/indics). We thank ACM India and Infosys for supporting the seminar.
