title: "Organizing the IndiCS seminar on concurrency"
date: "2026-09-28"
---

[Umang Mathur](https://umangmathur.org/) and [Krishna S.](https://www.cse.iitb.ac.in/~krishnas/) (IIT Bombay) are organizing **Frontiers in Verification and Analysis for Concurrency**, an ACM India IndiCS seminar at the **Infosys Mysore campus, 30 November–4 December 2026**. The seminar will bring researchers together for talks, open-problem sessions, and discussions on testing, analysis, and verification of concurrent systems.
