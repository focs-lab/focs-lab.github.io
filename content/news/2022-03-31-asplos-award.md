title: "ASPLOS Best Paper Award in NUS Computing"
date: 2022-03-31
---

Our work on tree clocks receives the **ASPLOS 2022 Best Paper Award**. [NUS Computing covers the paper and its authors](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/news/2022-best-paper-award-asplos/): Umang Mathur, Andreas Pavlogiannis, Hünkar Can Tunç, and Mahesh Viswanathan.
