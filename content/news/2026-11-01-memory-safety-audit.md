What a proof guarantees depends on what we ask it to prove. Our
[audit of AI-generated memory-safety proofs](/blog/memory-safety-audit/) explains why specifications matter,
using a checked example and findings from our audit of the Anthropic–Theorem
verification effort. Read the paper and [supporting evidence](https://github.com/focs-lab/memory-safety-proof-audit).
